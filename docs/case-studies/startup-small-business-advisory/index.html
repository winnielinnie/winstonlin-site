Startup and Small Business Advisory

Advise early-stage startups, SMBs, and family businesses across North America and Asia on product, growth, operations, and technology choices.

AdvisoryProduct contribution

The problem

Growing businesses rarely need abstract strategy alone. They need sharper positioning, better pricing logic, clearer operating rhythm, practical technology choices, and a way to connect day-to-day execution to the next stage of growth.

My contribution

Advised early-stage startups, SMBs, and family businesses on product positioning, pricing, growth strategy, operations, and technology decisions.

Helped teams clarify the offer, identify the actual operating bottleneck, and choose where technology or process change would create leverage.

The decisions

Smaller and growing businesses often mix product, sales, service design, finance, and operations into one messy problem, so the work has to find the real constraint before recommending a fix.

The useful answer is rarely just 'use more software.' It is deciding what the business should make clearer, repeat more reliably, price differently, or stop doing.

Where it landed

Created practical operating and growth recommendations for businesses across different stages, geographies, and business models.

Built a stronger investor/operator lens for evaluating markets, offers, customer behavior, and the systems a company needs as it scales.
